# Sprite credits

Every sprite in `library.json` is the project's own 8 by 8 drawing. A drawing may start from a reference whose licence allows it; each such
sprite is listed here with its source, its licence and what was changed. A sprite not listed was drawn from general knowledge of the thing
it shows, or generated (the letters, digits and simple shapes). No sprite starts from a game's textures, a brand or a logo (ADR-005 2.6).

Sources used so far:

- Noto Emoji, <https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji>. Images are under the Apache License 2.0 (a copy is in that repository's
  `LICENSE`); the fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Changed: each drawing is reduced to 8 by 8 pixels, mapped to the
  visualizer's palette, and redrawn by hand.
