# Sprite credits

Every sprite in `library.json` is the project's own 16 by 16 drawing. A drawing may start from a reference whose licence allows it; each such
sprite is listed here with its source, its licence and what was changed. A sprite not listed was drawn from general knowledge of the thing it
shows, or generated (the letters, digits and simple shapes). No sprite starts from a game's textures, a brand or a logo (ADR-005 2.6).

Sources used so far:

- Noto Emoji, <https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji>. Images are under the Apache License 2.0 (a copy is in that repository's
  `LICENSE`); the fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Each sprite below began as the emoji image reduced to 16 by 16 pixels and
  mapped to the visualizer's palette, with darker edge pixels in place of an outline, and was then retouched. They are modified works.
